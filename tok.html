Both tasks reward the same thing this app is built for: positions you have thought through, tested against real cases and other points of view.

Library

Real cases to think with, and the vocabulary to discuss them. Every case comes with what it shows about knowledge, and can be followed as a thread. Each links to Wikipedia as a starting point. For assessed work, follow the article's references to a source you can cite.

Printable flashcards. All 73 cases as double-sided A6 cards, four to an A4 sheet: what happened, why it matters, the area of knowledge connection and key takeaways.

Journal

Your own thinking, collected in one place. Understanding in TOK means having positions you have argued out for yourself, and these notes are the raw material for the essay and the exhibition.
