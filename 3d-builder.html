Controls
  • Drag a part onto the mat to add it
  • Drag a piece to move it
  • Drag the + beside a piece to lay a row of copies facing the same way
  • Double-click or double-tap a piece to delete it
  • In Play: drag a loose piece to throw it, tap to flick it
  • Drag empty space to look around
  • Two fingers: move together to slide the view, twist to spin, spread to zoom out, close to zoom in
  • Mouse: right-drag looks around, middle-drag slides the view, scroll zooms
  • R turn · T tip · Ctrl+D copy · Del delete · Space play
