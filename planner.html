🗓️ Weekly Planner

Plan the week around the unit

Your class timetable, ready each week with the fixed slots already in. Pick the year and unit and its lesson sequence, from tuning in to reflecting, is ready to drop into any period.

Overview
Start

Type straight into any cell. Pick a unit on each week to get its lessons. Customise moves periods, days and activities. Days down switches to the slide layout.

Customise mode. Drag ⠿ to move an activity, a period or a whole day. Drag a block from the library into any cell, or tap + Add. × removes. Changes save automatically in this browser.

1 · Start a week

+ New week carries over the fixed slots from last week (morning message, duties, specials, homework), moves the date on seven days and keeps the unit.

2 · Pick the unit

Choose the year and unit. You get the central idea, lines of inquiry, success criteria and the six-lesson sequence. Tick lessons, choose a period, Place.

3 · Share it

Copy table pastes the week into Word, Docs, PowerPoint or a spreadsheet. Print gives an A3 page per week.
