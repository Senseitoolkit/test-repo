Quick ways to plan

  • Tap to place: tap a block in the right-hand panel, then tap as many cells as you like. Tap the block again, or press Esc, to stop. Works on iPads.
  • Drag: drag blocks from the panel, or move any activity by its ⠿ handle.
  • Stretch: pull the orange bar on a cell's edge over more periods.
  • ⋯ on a cell: repeat it all week, copy it to next week, clear it, or block out the time.
  • → on an activity: move something you didn't get to into the same slot next week.
  • Notes & to-dos under each week: unfinished to-dos carry over to the next week.
  • Hidden notes: hover an activity and press ✎. An orange dot marks it. They stay off printouts and exports unless you tick "Include hidden notes".
  • Export ▾ on a week: copy an editable table for OneNote, or download Word, PowerPoint or PDF.
