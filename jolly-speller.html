Pick a group (the Jolly Phonics order), then a sound or All. Each test is 10 words, starting with ones not yet mastered. Tap a letter block or type on a keyboard; press Enter to check. With Hints on you get the sound, Peek, only the word's letters plus a few extras, and red-letter clues. Turn off Hints and Letter spaces for a harder test. Words spelled right on the first try turn green in the list. Words that sound like other words (see, blue, right) are read in a sentence.
