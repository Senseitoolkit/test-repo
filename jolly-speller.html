See the words for this test

The Paper button in the header has a printable test sheet (blue, dotted and red lines, with the teacher's words for the current test), every word list including the tricky word sets, and an a–z letter formation guide.

Pick a group (the Jolly Phonics order, then the tricky word sets), then a sound or All. Each test is 10 words, starting with ones not yet mastered. Tap a letter block or type on a keyboard; press Enter to check. With Hints on you get the sound, Peek, only the word's letters plus a few extras, and red-letter clues. Turn off Hints and Letter spaces for a harder test. Words spelled right on the first try turn green in the list. Words that sound like other words (see, blue, right) are read in a sentence.
