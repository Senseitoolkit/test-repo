Explore & Learn

Tap a card to open it. These tips will help you debate with confidence.

A debate is a structured discussion where two teams have different views on the same question. Each team explains why its view is best, using clear reasons and evidence.

It's a contest of ideas, not people. You win by thinking clearly, listening well, and speaking with purpose.

  • 🎤 Speakers — the team members who present ideas.
  • 🧑‍🏫 Moderator — usually the teacher. They keep time and make sure everyone takes turns.
  • 👏 Audience — the rest of the class. They listen carefully and vote at the end.

Use P-B-E to build a point:

P — Point
Say what you think.
"I think recess should be longer."
B — Because
Give your reason.
"Because students focus better after a break."
E — Example
Show proof or a story.
"For example, after playing I'm ready to read."
"I believe that…"
"I think… because…"
"My team agrees that…"
"I disagree because…"
"That's a fair point, but…"
"For example, …"
"Another reason is…"
"In conclusion, …"
  • 👂 Listen when the other team is speaking.
  • 🙋 Take turns — wait for your turn to talk.
  • 🧠 Challenge the idea, not the person. No put-downs.
  • 🤝 Be a good sport — say "good debate" at the end.
Opinion: What you think or feel.
Fact: Something true that can be checked.
Reason: The "because" that backs up your idea.
Evidence: Proof, like an example or a number.
Argument: Your idea plus your reasons (not a fight).
Rebuttal: When you answer the other team's idea.
For / Against: The two sides of the question.
Conclusion: Your final summary.
