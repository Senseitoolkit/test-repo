A Summer of Change

A two-week PYP summer school planner exploring the UN Sustainable Development Goals through inquiry, Daily 5, embedded math, visual thinking routines, and turn-and-talk. K1 to G5 — plus a generic, grade-agnostic template.

Tap a grade to jump. Use chevrons to fold cards away. Customise lets you move, add and remove pieces. Print opens a clean PDF of your version.

Customise mode. Drag ⠿ to move an activity, a session row or a whole day. Drag a block from the library into any cell, or tap + Add. × removes. Click any text to edit it. Changes save automatically in this browser.
Central idea: The SDGs are a call for action for every human who cares about the future of the Earth.

Weekly rhythm

3 inquiry days + 1 water-play day + 1 field-trip day. Flexible — hit Customise to drag days, sessions and activities where they fit best.

Two specials a day

PE, Art, ICT, Music, Library, Language — slot into any two of the six sessions.
