See the whole word list for this level

Tap a letter block or type on a keyboard. Press Enter to check. Use Full screen to hide everything except the test. For a harder game, turn on Random accents to hear each word in a different country's English (the country is the hint), or turn off Hints (no Peek, a full A–Z keyboard instead of just the word's letters, and no red-letter clues) and Letter spaces (no blanks showing how long the word is). Words you spell right on the first try turn green in the list. The National curriculum and Dolch word sets are kept separate, each with its own levels and progress. Each test is 10 words from the chosen level, starting with words you haven't mastered yet. Words that sound like other words (there, break, hour) are read in a sentence. The Paper button in the header has a printable 20 or 30 word test sheet (blue, dotted and red lines, with the teacher's words), the word lists for this set, and an a–z letter formation guide; both can be printed or saved as a PDF.
