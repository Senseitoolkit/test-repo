Tap a letter block or type on a keyboard. Press Enter to check. For a harder game, turn on Random accents to hear each word in a different country's English (the country is the hint), or turn off Hints (no Peek, a full A–Z keyboard instead of just the word's letters, and no red-letter clues) and Letter spaces (no blanks showing how long the word is). Words you spell right on the first try turn green in the list. Rounds are 10 words, picked with words you haven't mastered first.
