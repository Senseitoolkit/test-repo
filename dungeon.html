Text Based Dungeon Crawler

Eleven dungeons. Eleven beasts at the bottom. Fifty-four ways out, and a good many of them are graves.

  1. Choose a tale, then a hero. Eleven dungeons, each with its own monsters, boss and prize. Then pick a Warrior, Wizard, or Rogue. Each fights differently.
  2. Each turn, choose a move. Strike beats Trick. Trick beats Guard. Guard beats Strike.
  3. Read the tell. The monster shows a hint of its next move before you choose. It is usually true. Deeper monsters lie more.
  4. Win or lose, the story branches. Beat the monster and the path climbs. Fall near the surface and the dungeon drags you somewhere worse. Fall deep and you die.
