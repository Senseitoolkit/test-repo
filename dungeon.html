Text Based Dungeon Crawler

Eleven dungeons. Eleven beasts at the bottom. A hundred and twenty-four ways out, and more than half of them are graves.

  1. Choose a tale, then a hero. Eleven dungeons, each with its own monsters, boss and prize. Then pick a Warrior, Wizard, or Rogue. Each fights differently.
  2. Each turn, choose a move. Each one beats the next around the triangle.
  3. Read the tell. The monster shows a hint of its next move before you choose. It is usually true. Deeper monsters lie more.
  4. Win or lose, the story branches. Beat the monster and the path climbs. Fall near the surface and the dungeon drags you somewhere worse. Fall deep and you die. How you reach the end decides which ending you get.
