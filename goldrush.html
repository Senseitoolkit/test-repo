Forty-Niner's Gamble

California, 1848–1850. Dig for gold, sell shovels to the people who dig, or take a steady wage. Every choice costs you the other two.

How to play
1Pick a road each season: dig for gold, sell supplies, or work for pay.
2See what you gave up. The best road you skipped is your opportunity cost.
3Be richest in 1850. Want every number? Switch to Detailed in the bar above.
12 seasons, 3 roadsEach season, from Spring 1848 to Winter 1850, pick one: prospect for gold, trade supplies, or work for wages. You can only do one.
Opportunity costAfter each season the ledger shows what the best road you didn't take would likely have paid. That's your opportunity cost.
Supply & demandCamp prices rise when newcomers pour in (demand) and fall when ships unload crates (supply). Selling lots at once adds supply and pushes your own price down.
HypeRumors raise gold fever. Fever brings miners, which makes goods scarce and claims crowded. Some rumors are true. Some are hoaxes.
GoalEnd 1850 with the highest net worth: cash plus goods in your warehouse. You start with $300.
