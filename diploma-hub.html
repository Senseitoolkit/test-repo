IB Diploma Hub

The IB Diploma is six subjects plus a three-part core, taken over two years and scored out of 45. This page shows how the parts fit together and opens three tools: one for the whole picture and one each for the two core tasks that earn points.

Six subjects, graded 1 to 7: up to 42 pointsCore: up to 3
Six subjectsUp to 42 pointsFirst language, second language, humanities, science, mathematics, and an arts subject or elective. Three at Higher Level, three at Standard Level.
Theory of KnowledgeCore · graded A to EA course on how we know what we know. Assessed by an exhibition of three objects and a 1,600-word essay.
Extended EssayCore · graded A to EAn independent research essay of up to 4,000 words on a question the student chooses.
CASCore · no points · compulsoryCreativity, Activity, Service. Ongoing activities outside lessons. No diploma without it.
  • 45 is the maximum score
  • 24 is the minimum to pass
  • 3 core points come from the TOK and essay grades combined
  • An E in TOK or the essay means no diploma
