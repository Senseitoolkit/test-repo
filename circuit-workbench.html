Room20.0 °C

Drag a part onto the bench, or tap it to drop it in the middle. Drop a loose end onto another end to connect them. Double-tap a junction to split it apart.

Challenges

Build a machine that needs several functions working at once. Parts with too little power work badly, and parts with too much burn out. Finish without any burnouts or short circuits to earn 3 stars.
