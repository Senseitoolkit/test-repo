Parts of the test

Check the year, form and parts, then press Start test at the top. Teacher settings are under Menu. Press the corner icon during the test for full screen.

How it works
1Choose your year, form and parts.
2Press Start. Use full screen if you can.
3Answer each question. Skip only if you need to.
4Press End test when you finish.
In this test
SSentence completion: one word is missing from each sentence. Pick the word that fits the grammar and the meaning.
PPassage completion: three passages with numbered blanks (six each in Year 1, eight in Year 2, ten from Year 3). The right word depends on the whole passage.
Printable score sheet

One page per test: pupils copy their score for each part and their words to learn, then write what went well, what was hard and how they will improve.
