The Thinking ToolkitPYP · Reception to Year 6
Built on the Think · Challenge · Support · Routine planning routine and the IB PYP critical-thinking sub-skills, with developmental notes after Piaget, Vygotsky, Bandura and Erikson. Thinking routines are from Harvard Project Zero. Units are the KIST 2026–27 Programme of Inquiry. Practice notes draw on the EEF guidance on metacognition and CAST's UDL guidelines.
